      Method

      How it works

      01

      Discover

      Your usernames are checked on public platform APIs straight from your browser. TraceMe reads your GitHub commits, README and linked accounts, follows links between profiles, and pivots on any email it finds (Gravatar, reused handles).

      02

      Extract (NLP)

      Pattern matching and named-entity recognition pull out emails, phone numbers, locations, employers, schools, birth dates, family mentions, travel plans and sensitive topics.

      03

      Match entities

      Accounts are compared on cross-links, shared emails and websites, name similarity, location and TF-IDF bio similarity, so an account that only shares your username isn't counted as yours.

      04

      Classify risk

      Each exposure is rated by severity and mapped to threats: doxxing, identity theft, phishing, account takeover and stalking. Risky combinations of details are flagged too.

      Trust

      Privacy

      Public data only

      TraceMe never logs in or reads private data. Instagram, LinkedIn, X and similar sites are listed when you add them but never fetched.

      Nothing leaves your device

      There is no TraceMe server. Your browser talks directly to the public APIs, and the report exists only in this tab.

      For self-assessment

      Use TraceMe on your own accounts. Looking up other people without their consent may be illegal where you live.
