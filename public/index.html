      How it works

      1

      Discover

      Your usernames are checked on 15 platforms through their official public APIs, and links in your profiles are followed one hop to find connected accounts.

      2

      Extract (NLP)

      Pattern matching and named-entity recognition pull out emails, phone numbers, locations, employers, schools, birth dates, family mentions, travel plans and sensitive topics.

      3

      Match entities

      Accounts are compared on cross-links, shared emails and websites, name similarity, location and TF-IDF bio similarity, so an account that only shares your username isn't counted as yours.

      4

      Classify risk

      Each exposure is rated by severity and mapped to threats: doxxing, identity theft, phishing, account takeover and stalking. Risky combinations of details are flagged too.

      Privacy

      • Public data only. TraceMe never logs in, scrapes behind login walls, or touches private data. Instagram, LinkedIn, X and similar sites are listed when you add them but not fetched.
      • Nothing is stored. Scans run in memory and the report exists only in your browser. Close the tab and it's gone.
      • For self-assessment. Use TraceMe on your own accounts. Looking up other people without their consent may be illegal where you live.
